What we do

From archived records to forecasts your team runs

Forecast in real time

Why now

Monitoring was built for a climate that no longer exists

Quarterly grab samples were designed for a world that changed slowly. Today, watersheds shift faster than static monitoring can track, and the move to continuous, real-time data too often abandons decades of baseline records along the way.

We bridge that divide. OpenCurrent brings weather-style forecasting to water quality and quantity by anchoring modern machine learning in historical ground truth.

And we reject the black box. We build alongside our partners, coaching their teams to understand, run, and own the code they rely on.

What this looks like in practice

Satellite-to-stream water quality

Calibrating remote sensing algorithms against historical grab samples to map lake and reservoir dynamics across space and time.

Continuous sensor deployment

Designing and integrating in-stream sensor arrays that translate raw voltage into immediate decision support.

Predictive modeling workflows

Training catchment-scale models that forecast acute water-quality events, such as post-fire sediment runoff or bloom conditions, from upstream precipitation and weather forecasts.

Institutional coaching

Running hands-on sprints with municipal and watershed scientists to demystify automated workflows, version control, and AI-assisted coding tools.
